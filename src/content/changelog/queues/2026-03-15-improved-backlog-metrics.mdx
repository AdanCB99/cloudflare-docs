Previously, Cloudflare Queues backlog metrics included averabe backlog size (in bytes & number of messages) and average consumer lag time.

Now, you can access your backlog metrics in realtime:

- with GraphQL via `backlog_size`, `backlog_count`, and `backlog_lag_ms`.
- via the [Queues REST API](/api/resources/queues/methods/create/) through `env.QUEUE.getMetrics()`
- [in the dashboard](dash.cloudflare.com/?to=/:account/workers/queues/) by naviating into the **Metrics** tab of your queue
